## Release facts and rollback

- **Confirmed repair:** `hostinger/player-session.js` enters only a decoded title-start video gap of at most 0.25 s; the script URL was versioned. The measured deterministic startup delay dropped from 7.67–7.69 s to 1.72–2.12 s in separate Chrome runs. The intermittent 0.18-second stall is not claimed fixed.
- **Capacity:** No defensible Mac mini concurrent-viewer number exists. Cold four-rendition encoding took longer than the 12-second content duration; serving must use complete pre-transcoded packages. Mac mini CPU/RAM/disk behavior under 1/2/3/5 full viewers is unknown.
- **Current local previews:** `http://127.0.0.1:8765/adaptive-preview.html`, `http://127.0.0.1:8765/adaptive-preview.html?single=1`, and `http://127.0.0.1:8766/api/playback/preview`. They use a new **12-second** synthetic fixture, not the prior ten-minute fixture, and are local to this MacBook.
- **Branch and commits entering this classification:** `codex/desktop-streaming-production-base-20261023`; `5901c18e45a0d1b9bef4e9d90beaa456ffd969e7` (startup repair), `550ecc0de57b3652ebb2e66ff5a30705a6516e11` (readiness report and collector). Production frontend branch remained at `1f33c04888ef9235c9b882cee2b30779cdb348b9` in the prior push audit.
- **Staged deployment and rollback:** Preserve the exact Mac mini launcher, environment, cache, `server.js`, and capability module; recheck hashes. With separate approval, stage only the guarded capability and adaptive modules in an isolated directory with a private bounded cache. Install with `SV_ADAPTIVE_HLS_ENABLED=0`, restart under observation, verify existing routes/direct playback, then enable a limited canary. Roll back by disabling the flag, restoring the saved exact capability module, restarting under observation, and verifying original routes. No production change is authorized by this report.

**Final decision: NOT READY.** Mandatory real-media, Safari, actual mobile-data, production integration, and Mac mini capacity gates remain blocked or unresolved.
